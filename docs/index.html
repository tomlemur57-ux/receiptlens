Memos

Devnet is a test network, not proof of real payment. Transfer instructions are not net recipient credits. Rolled-back token instructions are omitted. Native deltas include fees and rent; unsafe-integer balances are omitted. Evidence boundary: ReceiptLens reports what public Solana RPC returned at inspection time. A finalized transaction is on-chain evidence, but the app does not identify real-world parties or decide whether a transfer satisfies a legal or commercial obligation.

WHY THIS EXISTS

Human-readable evidence

Raw explorer output is comprehensive but dense. ReceiptLens isolates facts useful for validating payments and automations.

Safe by construction

The endpoint is fixed to Devnet. There is no signer, key management, wallet connection, airdrop, simulation or transaction-submission code.
